1. Haversine BallTree Spatial Indexing

Groups multiple 375 m VIIRS sub-pixel fire detections inside overlapping 1 km MODIS ground footprints using spherical angular search radii, preventing duplicate wildfire alerts.

2. Radiometric FRP Inter-Calibration

3. Bayesian Confidence Unification

Converts categorical VIIRS flags (low, nominal, high) and continuous MODIS percentages (0–100%) into a single calibrated probability score boosted by multi-satellite coincidence.
